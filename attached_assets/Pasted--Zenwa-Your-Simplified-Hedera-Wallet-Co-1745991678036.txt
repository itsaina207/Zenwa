## 2. Problem & Solution

- **Problem** : Crypto wallets are often complex, scattered across multiple interfaces, and inaccessible to most users.
- **Solution** : Zenwa embeds a custodial wallet directly in Telegram, accessible everywhere, and lets you interact via buttons or natural language.

## 5. How It Works (3 Steps)

1. **Create your wallet**  
   Send `/start` then `/createwallet` to generate your Hedera account.
2. **Manage your assets**  
   Use `/balance`, `/send`, `/sendtoken`, or `/airdrop` via buttons or natural language.
3. **Query & Automate**  
   Ask "Who owns token 0.0.xxxx?" or "Am I eligible for an airdrop?" for instant answers.
